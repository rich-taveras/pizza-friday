What makes it different

Three things you will not find at a chain — or at most pizza shops:

The dough

48-hour sourdough, part whole wheat

No commercial yeast. Our dough is leavened only by a living starter — the same one behind our whole-wheat loaves — and cold-fermented for two days. The result: deep flavor, a blistered crust and a pizza that sits easy.

The special

A rotating Latin special

Every month, one special with roots: pernil and sweet plantain, sofrito sauce, the flavors of home — on a Neapolitan-style sourdough crust. When it sells out, it's gone until next month.

The ritual

One Friday drop, numbered pizzas

A limited batch each Friday, made to pre-order and baked minutes before pickup. Every pizza is numbered by batch — when the list fills, the week is closed. Small on purpose.

How it will work

Simple, small and planned ahead — like everything in our kitchen.

Order online by Thursday

Orders are online only. The Friday list opens early in the week and closes when the batch fills. Payment confirms your spot.

Baked Friday, to order

Your pizza goes into the oven minutes before your pickup slot — not before. Hot and blistered, never reheated.

Pickup in Chantilly

Pick up in your time window, take it home, and if there's a handwritten note in the box — that's on purpose.

FAQ

Are you open for orders?

Not yet. We are in the testing phase — baking for family, church and friends while we fine-tune the dough, the oven and the timing, and complete the required permits for selling food. Join the waitlist and you'll be the first to know when the first Friday opens.

Why only Fridays?

Great sourdough can't be rushed, and neither can we. One day a week keeps every batch small, fresh and consistent — and leaves room for everything else that matters.

Is the dough really 100% sourdough?

Yes — no commercial yeast. The dough is leavened with our starter and fermented about 48 hours, with a portion of whole-wheat flour in the blend.

Do you also bake bread?

Yes — the same starter feeds our whole-wheat sourdough loaves. A weekend bake box (Friday pizza + Saturday loaf) is in the plans.
